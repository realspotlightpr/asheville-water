# Installation and Buying-Guide Drafts 61–70

## 61. How to Size a Whole-House Water Filter
**Slug:** `/whole-house-water-filter-sizing/` · **Query:** whole house water filter sizing

Whole-house sizing begins with peak flow, not only household size. Count likely simultaneous fixtures, review pipe size and pressure, then match equipment service flow to demand. Water chemistry and contaminant load determine media volume and maintenance frequency. Undersizing can create pressure loss or inadequate contact time; oversizing adds cost and may create operational problems.

## 62. How to Size a Water Softener
**Slug:** `/water-softener-sizing/` · **Query:** what size water softener do I need

Softener sizing uses measured hardness, household water consumption, peak flow, and any compensated hardness required for certain iron conditions. Capacity claims should be evaluated alongside salt efficiency and regeneration settings. Correct sizing avoids both frequent regeneration and long stagnant intervals. A personalized calculation is more dependable than choosing by bedroom count alone.

## 63. Water Treatment Installation Timeline
**Slug:** `/water-treatment-installation-timeline/` · **Query:** how long water filter installation takes

Project timing includes water review, testing where needed, equipment selection, scheduling, installation, startup, and follow-up. A straightforward under-sink system differs from a multi-stage well-water project. Plumbing access, drain or power work, permits where applicable, and equipment availability can affect timing. A clear proposal should state what happens before, during, and after installation.

## 64. Plumbing Requirements for Water Treatment
**Slug:** `/water-treatment-plumbing-requirements/` · **Query:** whole house filter plumbing requirements

Whole-home equipment generally needs an appropriate main-line location, adequate pipe sizing, shutoff and bypass capability, pressure compatibility, and service clearance. Some systems need drainage or power. Placement should protect the home, avoid freezing, and permit maintenance. A licensed plumber can integrate equipment without creating an avoidable flow restriction or inaccessible installation.

## 65. Drain Requirements for Water Softeners
**Slug:** `/water-softener-drain-requirements/` · **Query:** water softener drain requirements

Regenerating softeners discharge water and need a properly designed drain route with an air gap or other protection required by applicable plumbing rules and manufacturer instructions. Drain height, distance, flow, freezing risk, and connection method matter. A loose hose pushed into an unsuitable drain is not a professional plan.

## 66. Electrical Requirements for Water Treatment
**Slug:** `/water-treatment-electrical-requirements/` · **Query:** does water treatment need electricity

Many control valves, UV systems, pumps, and reverse-osmosis accessories require power, while some cartridge housings do not. Confirm voltage, outlet location, moisture protection, backup behavior, and cord routing before installation. Electrical needs should be coordinated with plumbing placement so equipment remains safe and serviceable.

## 67. Water Treatment Warranty Questions
**Slug:** `/water-treatment-warranty-questions/` · **Query:** water treatment system warranty

Ask which components are covered, for how long, and whether labor, travel, media, cartridges, shipping, or water-damage claims are excluded. Learn what maintenance records are required and who handles service. A long headline warranty can be less valuable than clear local support and accessible replacement parts.

## 68. DIY vs. Professional Water Treatment Installation
**Slug:** `/diy-vs-professional-water-treatment-installation/` · **Query:** DIY whole house water filter installation

DIY may be reasonable for a simple manufacturer-approved cartridge change, but system selection and main-line plumbing carry higher stakes. Incorrect sizing, drainage, bypasses, pressure, sanitation, or cross-connections can undermine performance and reliability. Professional installation is particularly valuable for whole-home equipment, regenerating systems, and multi-stage well-water treatment.

## 69. Water Treatment for Rental Properties
**Slug:** `/rental-property-water-treatment/` · **Query:** water treatment for rental property

Rental-property systems should be durable, simple to maintain, and supported by documented schedules. Owners must distinguish health-related concerns from aesthetic preferences and follow applicable landlord obligations. Consider tenant instructions, filter access, leak protection, service responsibility, vacancy periods, and turnover inspections before selecting equipment.

## 70. Questions to Ask Before a Water Treatment Quote
**Slug:** `/water-treatment-quote-questions/` · **Query:** questions to ask water treatment company

Ask what test data supports the recommendation, what each stage is designed to do, how it is sized, which claims are independently certified, what maintenance costs, and who installs and services it. Request total installed scope, warranty details, operating requirements, and exclusions. A credible provider should explain when treatment is unnecessary or when more testing is needed.
